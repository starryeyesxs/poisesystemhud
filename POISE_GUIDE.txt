POISE SYSTEM - PLAYER GUIDE
===========================

Poise brings a true posture mechanic to Skyrim with no other poise or combat
mod required. Every hit chips away at an enemy's (or your own) balance, and
when it runs out they stagger.

We aim to stay compatible with combat frameworks and HUD mods such as BFCO,
Valhalla Combat, and TrueHUD, but that is a goal rather than a guarantee and
still needs testing with individual setups. If Chocolate Poise is detected,
built-in poise turns itself off to avoid competing with it.

This system is a work in progress. Balance, spell coverage, and compatibility
may change as testing continues. It requires Skyrim SE/AE, SKSE64, Address
Library, and PrismaUI. The poise hooks are currently validated only for
Skyrim 1.7.104.0.

HOW POISE WORKS
---------------
Poise is a separate posture resource for you and, when enabled, for loaded,
living NPCs and for creatures. The yellow bar shows your own poise. Hits lower
it; when it reaches zero the target staggers, poise refills, and a short grace
period protects against further poise breaks. A few seconds after the last hit,
poise regenerates over time. Other vanilla or scripted reactions can still
happen independently.

MAXIMUM POISE
Maximum poise starts at 40 and adds up:
  - The weight of your worn light and heavy armor, multiplied by
    fArmorWeightMultiplier (1.0 by default).
  - Shield weight multiplied by fShieldWeightPoiseMultiplier (0.0 by default,
    range -1.0 to 2.0). By default non-player actors instead use
    fArmorWeightMultiplier for shield weight; set bApplyShieldWeightToNPCs to
    false to use the shield multiplier for them too. Negative values reduce
    maximum poise, but the final total cannot fall below 10.
  - A Light Armor bonus: 0.15 per base armor-rating point of worn Light Armor,
    up to +15. Shields don't count toward this bonus.
  - Flesh spells: active Alteration armor spells such as Oakflesh add 0.45 per
    armor point they grant; Dragonhide adds a flat +60.
Flesh spell and Dragonhide poise is scaled by what you wear: any Heavy Armor
= 45%, otherwise any Light Armor = 75%, otherwise (unarmored or cloth) 100%.
Shields don't affect this check, so heavy and light armor is the trade-off:
Light Armor gains rating poise and keeps most of your flesh spell bonus,
Heavy Armor gains weight poise but loses more of the spell bonus.
Creatures scale only the base 40 by their tier; summoned and commanded allies
get x1.5 maximum poise.

PHYSICAL HITS
Melee hits, bashes, creature attacks, and arrows start from
8 + (weapon weight x 0.5) + a damage bonus. The bonus is 5% of the hit's
damage (sneak-attack multipliers included, before armor), capped at +6, so
big hits matter but can't dominate. That total is then multiplied by:
  - Attack type: two-handed x0.85, shield bash x1.0, weapon bash x0.35,
    creature attack x0.8 (times the creature's own tier scale), arrow x0.8.
  - Power attack: x1.5.
  - Scorched (see debuffs): x1.2 on non-arrow hits.
  - Unblocked hit: x2.5.
Blocking lets only a share of that through: x0.25 with a shield, x0.45 with a
weapon alone. Your Block skill, shield perks and well-timed parries shrink the
damage further on blocked hits. A ward alone does not reduce physical poise
damage.

Example: a 20-weight two-hander, unblocked power attack, 200 damage:
(8 + 10 + 6 capped) x 0.85 x 1.5 x 2.5 = about 76 poise damage.

SPELL HITS
Hostile fire, frost, and shock spell impacts damage poise, reduced by the
target's elemental and magic resistance. Channeled spells you hold down
(Flames, Frostbite, Sparks) deal NO direct poise damage by default, so poise
damage comes from impact spells: projectiles, beams and explosions. Their
status debuffs still apply, so Flames still sets enemies Scorched and
Frostbite still Numbs them. You can turn this on in the INI, in which case
channeled spells deal capped poise damage once per second. Cloak effects
work the same way.
A ward that blocks a spell takes most of the poise damage (x0.35). Blocking
with a shield or weapon also reduces it (x0.4 shield, x0.7 weapon), but a
ward takes precedence rather than stacking with them. Wards cost no extra
Magicka for this system. Not every spell or impact path is covered yet.
Spells cast by anyone but you deal x0.6 poise damage.

CREATURES
Creatures get poise by type (Light x0.75, Medium x1.7, Heavy x3.75, Boss x6.0
of base poise) and hit harder by type. After a creature's poise break it is
immune to another for 2 seconds, so you can't stunlock it, and bosses recover
1.5x faster and always play their full stagger. Wolves and skeevers are Light;
Draugr, Falmer and sabre cats are Medium; bears, trolls and werewolves are
Heavy; Dragons, Dragon Priests, Giants and Centurions are Boss.
Vanilla staggers are suppressed on actors using poise, so only poise breaks
stagger them. Shouts such as Unrelenting Force always keep their stagger.

POISE STATUS DEBUFFS
Hostile damage-over-time and drain effects don't hit poise directly; they slow
its recovery instead. They affect you, NPCs and creatures alike. Only hostile
effects count (an effect cast on yourself is ignored), the same debuff never
stacks, and different debuffs can be active together.
  Sapped        Hit by an absorb effect (Vampiric Drain, Absorb Health, or an
                absorb weapon enchantment, which holds it for about 2 seconds
                per hit). Poise recovery stops.
  Enfeebled     Poisoned. Poise only recovers up to a ceiling: 85% of maximum
                for the weakest poison, down to 70% for a strong one. No
                debuff can push the ceiling below 60%. Poise already above
                the ceiling isn't reduced, and a poise break still refills it.
  Numbed        Hit by lingering frost damage (Frostbite). Adds 1.0 second to
                the wait before poise starts to recover.
  Hemorrhaging  Bleeding: the War Axe perk's bleed, and bleed effects added by
                other mods. Poise recovers at 65% speed.
  Scorched      Burning from lingering fire damage (Flames). Whoever is burning
                takes x1.2 poise damage from melee and bash hits; arrows are
                unaffected.
Your poise bar shows what is affecting you: small icons appear under the bar,
a tick marks the Enfeebled ceiling, and the bar changes colour (Sapped violet,
Enfeebled green, Scorched orange, Numbed icy blue, Hemorrhaging crimson). The
bar stays visible while a debuff is active.

Poise is reset when loading a save and may reset when toggling the system.

SETTINGS
--------
Edit the installed Data\SKSE\Plugins\poisesystemhud.ini, then press F10 in game to
reload settings. Missing or invalid values retain their previous value and are
logged. true/false settings can also use 1/0. Defaults are shown below.

[Poise] - gameplay
bEnableBuiltInPoise=true          Enable the built-in player and NPC system.
bEnableNPCPoise=true              Enable poise for eligible NPCs.
bSuppressVanillaStaggers=true    Drop vanilla staggers on poise-managed actors.
bAllowTimedBashStaggers=false    Let the vanilla stagger from a timed bash through.
bEnableCreaturePoise=true         Enable creature poise pools and stagger suppression.
fLightCreaturePoiseMultiplier=0.75   Light creatures (wolves, skeevers, mudcrabs) base poise scale.
fMediumCreaturePoiseMultiplier=1.7   Medium creatures (draugr, falmer, chaurus, sabre cats) scale.
fHeavyCreaturePoiseMultiplier=3.75   Heavy creatures (bears, trolls, werewolves) scale.
fBossCreaturePoiseMultiplier=6.0     Boss creatures (dragons, giants, centurions) scale.
fBossCreatureRegenMultiplier=1.5     Boss creature poise regeneration multiplier.
fLightCreatureDamageMultiplier=0.6   Outgoing poise damage scale for light creatures.
fMediumCreatureDamageMultiplier=1.2  Outgoing poise damage scale for medium creatures.
fHeavyCreatureDamageMultiplier=1.375 Outgoing poise damage scale for heavy creatures.
fBossCreatureDamageMultiplier=2.25   Outgoing poise damage scale for boss creatures.
fCreatureStaggerImmunitySeconds=2.0  Seconds a creature cannot be poise-broken again.
fSummonPoiseMultiplier=1.5           Maximum poise scale for summoned and commanded actors.
fNPCMagicPoiseMultiplier=0.6         Magic poise scale for spells not cast by the player.
fBasePoise=40.0                   Starting maximum poise.
fArmorWeightMultiplier=1.0        Maximum poise per unit of worn armor weight.
fShieldWeightPoiseMultiplier=0.0 Player shield weight multiplier, range -1.0 to 2.0.
bApplyShieldWeightToNPCs=true    NPCs use fArmorWeightMultiplier for shield weight when true;
                                 when false they use fShieldWeightPoiseMultiplier.
fBaseHitPoiseDamage=8.0          Base physical hit poise damage.
fWeaponWeightDamageMultiplier=0.5 Additional damage per weapon-weight unit.
fPhysicalDamagePoiseMultiplier=0.05 Share of the hit's physical damage added to base damage.
fPhysicalDamagePoiseCap=6.0      Hard cap on that physical-damage bonus.
fTwoHandedMultiplier=0.85        Two-handed melee damage multiplier.
fShieldBashMultiplier=1.0         Bash multiplier when a shield is equipped.
fWeaponBashMultiplier=0.35        Bash multiplier for a weapon bash without a shield.
fCreatureMultiplier=0.8          Hit multiplier for attacks by non-NPC creatures.
fProjectileMultiplier=0.8        Physical projectile multiplier.
fPowerAttackMultiplier=1.5       Power attack multiplier.
fShieldBlockedPhysicalMultiplier=0.25 Physical hit multiplier when blocking with a shield.
fWeaponBlockedPhysicalMultiplier=0.45 Physical hit multiplier when blocking with a weapon only.
fUnblockedPhysicalMultiplier=2.5 Unblocked physical hit multiplier.
fRegenDelaySeconds=3.0           Delay after damage before regeneration.
fRegenRatePerSecond=8.0          Poise restored each gameplay second.
fStaggerImmunitySeconds=1.0      Grace period after a poise break.
fLightArmorRatingPoiseMultiplier=0.15 Maximum poise per base armor-rating point of worn Light Armor (no shields).
fLightArmorRatingPoiseCap=15.0   Hard cap on the Light Armor rating bonus.
fLightArmorFleshMultiplier=0.75  Flesh spell and Dragonhide scale while wearing Light Armor.
fHeavyArmorFleshMultiplier=0.45  Same while wearing Heavy Armor; takes priority over Light.
fFleshSpellPoiseMultiplier=0.45   Maximum poise per active Alteration armor point.
fDragonhidePoiseBonus=60.0       Flat maximum poise bonus for Dragonhide.
fMagicBurstPoiseMultiplier=2.0   Burst spell magnitude multiplier.
fConcentrationPoiseMultiplier=1.0 Concentration spell magnitude multiplier.
fConcentrationIntervalSeconds=1.0 Pooling interval for concentration spells (only if enabled).
fConcentrationMaxPoisePerInterval=15.0 Maximum poise damage per interval (only if enabled).
fWardMagicDamageMultiplier=0.35  Confirmed ward interception multiplier.
fShieldBlockedMagicMultiplier=0.4 Magic poise multiplier while blocking with a shield.
fWeaponBlockedMagicMultiplier=0.7 Magic poise multiplier while blocking with a weapon.

[Magic Poise] - spell damage rules
bConcentrationSpellsDealPoise=false  Channeled spells (Flames, Frostbite, Sparks) deal no
                                 direct poise damage; their debuffs still apply.

[Poise Debuffs] - status debuffs
bEnablePoiseDebuffs=true         Master switch for all five debuffs.
bEnableSappedDebuff=true         Individual switches for Sapped, Enfeebled, Numbed,
bEnableEnfeebledDebuff=true      Hemorrhaging and Scorched.
bEnableNumbedDebuff=true
bEnableHemorrhagingDebuff=true
bEnableScorchedDebuff=true
fEnfeebledRegenCeilingMin=0.70   Recovery ceiling for a strong poison.
fEnfeebledRegenCeilingMax=0.85   Recovery ceiling for the weakest poison.
fEnfeebledMagnitudeForMin=10.0   Poison damage per second that counts as strong.
fMinGlobalRegenCeiling=0.60      No debuff lowers the ceiling below this.
fNumbedRegenDelayBonus=1.0       Extra seconds before recovery while Numbed.
fHemorrhagingRegenRateMultiplier=0.65 Recovery speed while Hemorrhaging.
sHemorrhagingEffectKeywords=bleed,hemorrhag  Words that mark a mod's effect as a bleed.
fScorchedMeleeDamageMultiplier=1.20 Poise damage taken from melee while Scorched.

[HUD] - poise display
bShowPoiseBar=true               Show the poise bar; does not disable the system.
bShowPoiseNumbers=false          Show current / maximum poise on the bar.
bShowTargetPoiseBar=false        Bar for the last enemy you hit (name, type, poise).
bShowPoiseDebuffIcons=true       Debuff icons and ceiling tick on the poise bar.

See README.md for setting ranges, installation, HUD layout options and
compatibility details. F10 is the fixed reload key and is not configurable in
the INI. Tested alongside the CC F10 menu; they fire together with no issues.
